# Worked examples of Plot Constraint Mapping (all made up)

**Every work, character, place and event in this folder is invented** (author's direction, 2026-11-27). None of it belongs to the author's real works or to the Tepenian universe, and none of it is canon. The examples exist only to show the method (Derivation Mechanisms, `Stories/Plot_Constraints/Plot_Constraint_Mapping_Method.md`) in action. They replace the copies of the author's real worked cases that were here; those stay in their own repos.

| Example | Medium | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `A - The Glassblower's Winter/` | Stand-alone novel, about 300 pages | One protagonist's three load-bearing beats, built from her character data; a Midpoint menu; the status check and candidates kept in a separate analysis file; the audience pass held back until the outline stands |
| `B - The Salt Crown trilogy/` | Novel series: three books, each printed in two volumes | Layers (series, book, volume); shared constraints written once; continuity limits; a series beat and a book beat on the same point, declared separately and satisfied together (writing rule 6); a delivery division carrying a small requirement |
| `C - Night Ferry/` | Audio series, 12 episodes, adapted from an invented radio play | [Medium] and [Source] constraints; episodes that are both their own story units and parts of the series' structure; episode endings as delivery divisions |

**Kept short on purpose.** Real sets cover more beats and carry more items per beat. These cover the load-bearing beats (the two Doorways and the Midpoint) and a few others, which is where the method says to start (Step 2).
