# The Salt Crown trilogy: what every book shares (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

**Not the beats themselves.** These hold for each of the three books, judged as its own story unit. A book file adds to them and never repeats them. Tags: see `README.md`.

## Rules that hold in every book
1. **Each book has a protagonist whose choices the Doorways are** [Composite].
2. **Each book's Midpoint turns on that book's own antagonist** [Adopted].
3. **Volume 1 ends on a hook,** shortly after the book's Midpoint: a delivery-division requirement, not a beat [Adopted].

## Doorway #1 (~20%, p. ~80)
- **MUST:** a choice that commits the protagonist to this book's conflict [Bell].
- **CANNOT:** the same event as the previous book's Doorway #2 [Composite].

## Midpoint (50%, p. ~200)
- **MUST:** the stakes rise against this book's antagonist [Snyder].
- **MUST:** falls before the volume split, never after it [Adopted].
- **CANNOT:** this book's conflict resolved [Snyder].

## Doorway #2 (~80%, p. ~320)
- **MUST:** the protagonist's answer to this book's low point [Composite].
- **CANNOT:** the antagonist's move standing as the beat with no answer attached [Composite].
