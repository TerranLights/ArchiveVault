# The Salt Crown trilogy: the series layer (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

**Not the beats themselves.** Series beats only, judged against the series. Where a book beat shares the point, its own constraints are in the book file, and the event there must meet both. Tags: see `README.md`.

**Working from (established):** series protagonist Dara Venn (Enneagram Type Eight, adjusted to her: she trusts only what she can hold by force, and her flaw is treating every ally as a debt). Series opponent: the inland duke. The crown is recovered in Book 3. Archetype arcs: two across the trilogy (the length supports more than one).

## Continuity: cannot be true before its time
1. **The crown back in the marsh before Book 3** [Continuity].
2. **Dara knowing the duke ordered the theft before the series Midpoint** [Continuity].
3. **The tax-farmer acting in Book 1** [Continuity].

## Doorway #1 (~20%, Book 1, p. ~240)

### MUST
1. **Dara commits to taking the crown back,** by her own choice [Composite; Bell].
2. **The commitment costs her the smuggling trade** that kept her free of everyone [CDM].

### CANNOT
1. **Another smuggler's act standing as her crossing** [Composite].

## Midpoint (50%, Book 2, p. ~200)

### MUST
1. **The war for the crown changes in kind.** Before it, Dara fights the magistrate's men; after it, she knows the duke is the enemy [Snyder; Truby].
2. **Dara sees what her way of fighting has cost** the people who helped her [Bell; CDM].

### CANNOT
1. **The crown recovered, or within reach** [Continuity].

**Same point, two layers:** this is also Book 2's own Midpoint. Book 2's constraints are declared in `book-2.md`, separately. The event chosen for this point must meet these and those.

### MUST

### CANNOT
1. **A rescue by coincidence** [Law].
