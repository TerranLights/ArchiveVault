# The Salt Crown trilogy: plot constraints (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

**Not the beats themselves.** What each beat must and must not be. No file here says what happens.

**The work (invented):** a trilogy of novels. The marsh-folk's Salt Crown is taken by the inland duke; Dara Venn, a salt-marsh smuggler, carries the struggle to win it back across three books. Each book has its own antagonist: the harbor magistrate (Book 1), the duke's tax-farmer (Book 2), the duke himself (Book 3).

## Layers (Step 0)
- **Story units:** the trilogy (the series), and each book.
- **Delivery division:** each book is printed in two volumes, of about 210 and 190 pages. A volume has no beats of its own, but **Volume 1 must end on a hook** [Adopted].
- **Each layer's constraints are declared separately.** Where a series beat and a book beat fall on the same point, the one event there must satisfy both sets (writing rule 6).
