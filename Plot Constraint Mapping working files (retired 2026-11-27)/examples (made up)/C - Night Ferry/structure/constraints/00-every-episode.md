# Night Ferry: what every episode shares (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

**Not the beats themselves.** These hold for each of the 12 episodes, judged as its own story unit. Where an episode also carries a series beat, that beat's constraints are in `00-series.md`, declared separately, and the event there must meet both. Tags: see `README.md`.

## Rules that hold in every episode
1. **One crossing per episode** [Source].
2. **The passenger list is read aloud near the start** [Source].
3. **Each episode has a small arc of its own:** one passenger's story, opened and closed within the crossing [Adopted].
4. **The episode ends on an unanswered question** (the delivery-division requirement) [Adopted].

## The episode's own turn (around its middle)
- **MUST:** turns on something heard: a voice on the radio, a sound below deck, a silence [Medium].
- **MUST:** Marek decides something about the passenger of the episode [CDM].
- **CANNOT:** a turn that needs the listener to see anything [Medium].

## The episode's ending
- **MUST:** the passenger's small story is closed [Adopted].
- **MUST:** a new question is left open for the series [Adopted].
- **CANNOT:** the question answered within the same minute it is raised [Proposed].
