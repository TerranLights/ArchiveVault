# Night Ferry: the series layer (MADE-UP EXAMPLE)

**Not the beats themselves.** Series beats only. Tags: see `README.md`.

**Working from (established):** lead Marek Holm (Enneagram Type Five, adjusted to him: he would rather listen to the world through a headset than step into it; flaw, withholding what he knows). Opponent: undecided, deliberately deferred. One archetype arc.

## Doorway #1 (~20%, Ep. 3)

### MUST
1. **Marek chooses to act on what he has heard,** not only to log it [Composite; CDM].
2. **The choice is audible:** said aloud to someone, or made by a sound the listener can't mistake, such as the radio switched to transmit [Medium].

### CANNOT
1. **A choice shown only by a look or a gesture** [Medium].
2. **The ferry making a second crossing in one night** to set it up [Source].

## Midpoint (50%, end of Ep. 6)

### MUST
1. **The question changes from who the passenger is to why the ferry keeps carrying him** [Snyder; Proposed].
2. **It falls in the episode's last minutes,** so the episode's ending hook and the Midpoint are the same moment [Adopted].

### CANNOT
1. **A reveal that depends on the listener seeing a face, a mark or a photograph** [Medium].
2. **The captain leaving the bridge** to deliver it [Source].

### Menu of shapes (options, not a recommendation)
- **False victory:** Marek finds the passenger's name on an old list. *Additionally requires:* the list is read aloud, as the original reads the passenger list [Source].
- **False defeat:** the radio goes silent mid-crossing. *Additionally requires:* the silence itself is long enough to be heard as an event [Medium].

## Doorway #2 (~80%, Ep. 10)

### MUST
1. **Marek tells what he has withheld,** breaking his flaw, as his answer to All Is Lost [CDM; Composite].

### CANNOT
1. **The deferred opponent forced into the open by this constraint.** Who the opponent is stays the author's decision [Adopted: deferred by the author].

## Open Threads
