# Overprocess Summary Catalog

## ULM

#### Universal Location Methodology

The procedure for building any location in the Tepenian universe, from a single room to a whole city, so that it is a place somebody could actually live in. It governs every location type and scale, and it nests: a district inherits from its city, a room from its building. Its governing law is **depth over speed**: research instead of recall, chase each finding's effects three orders deep, and never produce a location just because it is next in line. *(Method: Derivation Mechanisms, `Environment/ULM [Universal Location Methodology]/`, starting at `00_RUNBOOK.md`.)*

#### Objective

The full location corpus for the Tepenian Federation (every city, every subnet, Concordia's districts, the orbital infrastructure, and later Mars), each place deep enough that the people, problems and stories downstream of it are not shallow.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **Inputs first:** the input contract (`05_The_Input_Contract.md`) lists what must be supplied before a pass, including all eight generators.
- **Frame the location:** its type, scale, inhabitation and time frame, and what it inherits from the places it sits inside (`01_Frame_Typology_and_Inheritance.md`).
- **The eleven phases** (`03_The_Phase_Spine.md`): 0 Frame; 1 Constraint and Capability; 2 Composition and Arrival; 3 Surface and Texture; 4 Ordinary Life; 5 Relation and Geometry; 6 Meaning; 7 Order; 8 Making; 9 Populations; 10 Catalog. Each phase checks its canon targets first.
- **Quality gates and sibling differentiation** (`04_QA_Gates_and_Differentiation.md`): the gates confirm a pass isn't wrong, and the differentiation check makes sure two sibling places don't come out alike.

## DCI

#### Deep Culture Investigation

A full, in-depth investigation of how each culture-nation that contributes people to the Tepenian Federation understands every concept of life, researched one regional culture or Indigenous people (a "unit") at a time and **on each culture's own terms**. The twelve zodiac signs are used only as research categories (each sign is a domain with its concepts). Culture is the long-lived layer, lasting centuries and sometimes millennia; law is researched only inside Libra (the letter of the law) and Capricorn (the spirit of the law). *(Method: Derivation Mechanisms, `Environment/DCI [Deep Culture Investigation]/`, starting at `RUNBOOK.md`; results in the Culture Library repo.)*

#### Objective

A full-depth cultural record of every contributing culture-nation, as the ground for the synthesis of the Federation's municipal and local cultures, and later for the Human-to-Robot DCI translation.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **Springboard scouting** (done for 53 of 74 culture-nations by 2026-11-26): for each nation, one starting-point sheet per concept, written from memory and marked *check*; where the research begins, never where it stops.
- **Stage 0, Scope:** the nation's territory and its units (regional cultures and Indigenous peoples), weighted founders first; the author settles the choices.
- **Stages 1 to 3, per unit:** fifteen lineage profiles (where each part of the culture comes from: family, kinship, religion, livelihood, land, political culture, education, language, stories, song, formative events, death and the dead, arts and craft, healing and the body, community life); measured data from surveys and censuses; Woodard's five axes as a first reading.
- **Stages 4 and 5, sign by sign from Aries:** each concept in each unit through the investigation sheet's 22 families of evidence, founders first, variation required; then the units combined into the nation's dominant view, shared floor, named outliers and confidence.
- **Stages 6 and 7:** how 2083, statehood and the time since changed each reading (derivation; the author decides); then verification of every output.

## CST

#### Cultural Synthesis Techniques

A generative toolkit for building the culture of one specific place: a Concordia district, an outer Tepenian city, or any future location. Each technique is **a question with a structure, not a result to reproduce**: run through the same technique, two places must come out unlike each other, or one of them is wrong. Its sibling, RWBEM, supplies the raw material; CST supplies the operations performed on it. *(Method: Derivation Mechanisms, `Environment/CST [Cultural Synthesis Techniques]/Cultural_Synthesis_Techniques.md`.)*

#### Objective

The full CST corpus for the Tepenian Federation: a distinct, believable local culture for every city, district and other location, built from what was actually researched about it, and felt by a player moving through the place.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **The governing filter, characteristic plausibility,** and the player-facing test: what a player would actually meet in the space.
- **Where the answers come from:** each technique draws on the location's completed ULM pass, through the per-technique source map.
- **The techniques themselves,** applied one by one (for example the Bounded Personal Franchise, the Failure State of the Core Value, the Universal Micro-Practice, Native Before Transplanted, the Population Share Check and the Composition Merge), each with a divergence table showing unlike results across kinds of place.
- **The binding law on origin:** composition names the stock; time and place produce the culture. Origin is ancestry, not identity.

## RWBEM

#### Real-World Basis Extrapolation Method

The method for researching the real places that each Tepenian location is based on (its basis picks) and extrapolating from them into the location. Its governing law is **research fully**: a pick is not exhausted because it has been searched, since the research is the input data that everything downstream inherits. *(Method: Derivation Mechanisms, `Environment/RWBEM [Real-World Basis Extrapolation Method]/Real-World_Basis_Extrapolation_Method.md`.)*

#### Objective

The full RWBEM corpus for the Tepenian Federation: real, accurate input data behind every location, so that its realism and accuracy rest on what real places are actually like.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **Step A, the full pick list:** every Primary, Secondary and Supporting basis pick for the location, not just the Primary ones.
- **Step B, research each pick as a real, specific place,** following every thread a source opens, with no time limit and no search budget.
- **Step C, the fusion question,** asked for each concrete detail found (not each pick): what would this become in the Tepenian location?
- **Steps D and E:** cross-check against everything already established before writing anything, then write the result into the right target by its own conventions, logging every open thread noticed but not chased.
