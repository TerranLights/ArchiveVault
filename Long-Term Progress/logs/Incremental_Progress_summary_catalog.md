## Hawaiian/Tepenian language

#### Modern Hawaiian, which becomes Tepenian

The language of the Tepenian universe. Over the roughly 500 years between the Great War (2083) and the Falkland Treaty (2564), Modern Hawaiian takes shape as a complete language, starting as a pidgin of English, Japanese and Tagalog on the islands of Hawaii (canon C-2, C-4). Over the Second Interwar (2564 to 2812), influenced by many more languages, it evolves into a unified language, Tepenian (C-13).

#### Objective

A complete, working language at two targets: Hawaiian at the Falkland Treaty (2564) and Classical Tepenian at 2812, the main target (D-21). Published as a series of eBooks: a grammar reference, a full dictionary and a couple of history books; later, public-domain books translated into it.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **The repo's roadmap** (`Reference/TepenianLanguage/ROADMAP.md`), Phases 0 to 11: foundation; source acquisition; contributing-language analysis; contact-language modeling; the layering table; stage-by-stage derivation.
- **Then the build:** the core language; lexicon expansion; the history books; eBook assembly.
- **Hand-off to slang synthesis,** then public-domain translations once the language works.
- **Stage 1, Groundwork** (2026-11-25): Phase 0 in progress, decisions D-1 to D-27 recorded.

## Human-to-Robot DCI translation

#### Robot DCI datasets

Work out how to analyze the DCI's results for each real-world human culture-nation and translate them into robot counterparts: how robots *from* those societies would understand the same concepts. Highly theoretical; expected to take at least a year, most likely a year and a half to two.

#### Objective

A set of synthesized Robot DCI datasets, each comparably equivalent to its human counterpart, kept with the culture results in the Culture Library.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **The human side:** each completed human culture-nation dataset, with its after-the-fact analysis against Brown's *Human Universals* (only after that nation's research is complete; never during it).
- **The robot side:** the Robot Universals synthesized for this universe (`Characters/Robot-specific/Robot_Universals/`), extrapolated out to a robot culture-nation dataset, with the human dataset as the guide.
- **Depends on** the DCI's full research for each culture-nation. Its own process is separate from the GDD's robot-culture files, which are an unfinished foundation. *(Project file: Derivation Mechanisms, `Environment/DCI [Deep Culture Investigation]/LONG-TERM PROJECT - Robot DCI datasets.md`.)*

# Intermittent Priority

## Particulars of regional standards

#### Particulars of regional standards

Which aspects of Tepenian national culture each regional culture established, for each regional culture, subnet and area: "whoever names it first, usually names it for good." The author's example: Tepenia's radio and communications network was first built by Australians, so Australian Comms protocol became the national protocol, and much of the national communications vocabulary comes from Australian slang. The author's full note is in `../README.md` and in the author's list, word for word.

#### Objective

For every regional culture, subnet and area, the national standards it set, and what of that culture each carries.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **Which culture established which thing:** for each system, institution, practice or norm of the national culture, which regional culture set it first. Each case is the author's to decide.
- **The cultures doing the shaping:** the full DCI research on each contributing culture shows what it brought; the synthesis of each city's local culture is where regional and national standards meet.
- **The vocabulary trail,** through slang synthesis and the Tepenian language.

## Slang synthesis

#### Slang synthesis

In-universe slang for the universe's fictional languages and cultures, grounded in how slang actually forms in real human languages rather than invented arbitrarily (`Reference/slang-synthesis/`).

#### Objective

Plausible slang for each in-universe culture and language, handed on to the Tepenian language where it belongs.

#### Summary of Methodology

- **Reference extraction** from a large local library of real slang sources, language by language. Phase 1 extraction was complete for sixteen languages by 2026-11-04: Serbian/Croatian/Bosnian, Hungarian, Korean, Russian, Spanish, Dutch, Romanian, Japanese, Tagalog, English, Hawaiian, Ilocano, Cantonese, Swedish, Norwegian and Danish. German, Italian and Mongolian are triaged, not yet extracted. The current list is in the repo's `LANGUAGE_INDEX.md`.
- **Analysis** of how slang forms in each.
- **Synthesis** of the in-universe slang, with hand-offs to the Tepenian language repo.
