## Notes on 01

Supersedes an earlier version (`01_kosoado_deictic_territory.svg`, deleted) whose first panel set an
"English" box beside a "日本語" box. The territorial finding survived the rebuild; the English frame
did not. Changes made in the rebuild:

- **ど is not treated as an "interrogative."** That is an English word-class import. Japanese-internally
  ど is the cell where the domain is *not yet assigned* — which is why it is the fourth row of the
  same paradigm, generated by the same suffixes, rather than a separate system. The system names
  itself by its four values (こ・そ・あ・ど), which is the clue.
- **The three fields are the structure**, not a spatial system with "extensions."
- **場③ 心理 is flagged as unsolved, not drawn as settled.** Kodansha (pp.11–13, 30) explicitly
  states that assignment on this field is *not* transparently predictable from surface distance.
  This is where the next Japanese diagram should go — it is also the slang-relevant layer.
- **知りません / 分かりません** is cited as Japanese-internal corroboration that "whose side does
  this sit on?" is a recurring question in the grammar, not a frame imposed on こそあど.

## Notes on 02

The directional formulation is the user's, and it is tighter than the sources', which list は and が
as two labels ("already-known topic" vs. "new/emphasized information"). One mechanism with two
directions explains why the pair exists and predicts two facts the label account only enumerates:
WH-subjects and their answers both taking が, and は being barred from subordinate clauses. That
second prediction is an extension of the logic, not a claim any source makes directly.

**Scope limit:** the directional account covers the focus contrast. It does not obviously reach
double-が (水が飲みたい), relative-clause subjects, or the conjunction が ("but"). The diagram is
scoped accordingly rather than overreaching.
