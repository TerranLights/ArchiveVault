## Findings

### Founding Population and When (DCI line 01)
- The **Dene** **peoples'** **territories** were **occupied **for** **thousands** **of years** **before** **European** **contact** — the **Dene** **language** **family** **is** **part** **of** **the** **Na-Dene** **stock**, **with** **the** **Dene** **speakers** **having** **migrated** **from** **Siberia** **across** **the** **Bering** **Land** **Bridge** **at** **least** **10,000** **years** **before** **European** **contact**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **territory** **in** **the** **interior** **of** **British** **Columbia**, **the** **Yukon**, **and** **Alberta**, **straddling** **the** **northern** **Rocky** **Mountains** **and** **the** **interior** **plateaus**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **organized** **into** **bands** **and** **tribes** **with** **a** **loose** **political** **structure** **based** **on** **kinship** **and** **chiefs** **selected** **from** **prominent** **families**, **with** **no** **centralized** **authority**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **known** **for** **their** **use** **of** **the** **twine**, **the** **net**, **and** **the** **basket** **— **the** **Dene** **were** **skilled** **basket** **makers**, **with** **the** **Dakelh** **being** **known** **for** **their** **elaborate** **basket** **work**, **and** **the** **Gwich'in** **being** **known** **for** **their** **caribou** **hunting** **and** **the** **trapper** **culture**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **interaction** **with** **the** **interior** **of** **British** **Columbia** **and** **the** **Yukon** **— **their** **culture** **is** **defined** **by** **the** **fish** **runs**, **the** **caribou** **herds**, **the** **game**, **and** **the** **forest**.

### The Governing Frame (DCI line 02)
- The **Dene** **culture** **is** **defined** **by** **a** **loose** **form** **of** **politicism** **rather** **than** **a** **centralized** **authority** — **the** **Dene** **peoples** **lived** **in** **bands** **and** **tribes** **with** **a** **loose** **political** **structure** **based** **on** **kinship** **and** **chiefs** **selected** **from** **prominent** **families**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **interaction** **with** **the** **fish** **runs**, **the** **caribou** **herds**, **the** **game**, **and** **the** **forest** — **their** **subsistence** **was** **based** **on** **fishing**, **hunting**, **and** **gathering**, **with** **the** **Dakelh** **being** **known** **for** **their** **salmon** **fishing**, **the** **Gwich'in** **for** **their** **caribou** **hunting**, **and** **the** **Tlicho** **for** **their** **caribou** **hunting** **and** **fishing**.
- The **Dene** **peoples'** **religion** **was** **characterized** **by** **belief** **in** **a** **supreme** **creator**, **faith** **in** **dreams**, **and** **use** **of** **song** **narratives** **in** **ritual** **and** **ceremony** — **the** **Dene** **religion** **was** **deeply** **rooted** **in** **the** **natural** **world** **— **the** **animals**, **plants**, **rocks**, **and** **water** **sources** **were** **all** **believed** **to** **have** **spirits** **or** **personalities**.
- The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **interaction** **with** **the** **interior** **of** **British** **Columbia** **and** **the** **Yukon** — **their** **culture** **is** **a** **culture** **of** **hunting**, **fishing**, **and** **gathering**, **with** **the** **Dakelh** **being** **known** **for** **their** **salmon** **fishing**, **the** **Gwich'in** **for** **their** **caribou** **hunting**, **and** **the** **Tlicho** **for** **their** **caribou** **hunting** **and** **fishing**.

### Territory Within the Target (DCI line 03)
- The I6 unit is the **Dene** **speaking** **peoples'** **territory** — the **interior** **of** **British** **Columbia**, **the** **Yukon**, **and** **Alberta**, **straddling** **the** **northern** **Rocky** **Mountains** **and** **the** **interior** **plateaus**.
- Bounded by the **Plateau** **peoples** (I2) **to** **the** **southwest**, the **Great Basin edge** **peoples** (I5) **to** **the** **west**, and the **settler** **units** **of** **the** **BC** **interior** **and** **El** **Norte** (S4) **to** **the** **south** **and** **southeast**.

### Present-Day Demographics (DCI line 04)
- The **Dene** **peoples** **today** **are** **politically** **organized** **into** **band/tribe** **governments** (the **Dakelh** (Carrier), **Sahtu** (Hare), **Gwich'in**, **Tlicho** (Dogrib), **Déné** **Sulpiqué** (Beaver)) **with** **land** **claims** **and** **self** **government** **agreements** **being** **a** **major** **political** **feature** **of** **the** **region**.
- The **Indigenous** **population** **is** **concentrated** **in** **the** **reservations** **and** **rural** **communities** (the **Dakelh** **reservations** **in** **British** **Columbia**, **the** **Sahtu** **Dene** **of** **the** **Sahtu** **Region** **in** **Northwest** **Territories**, **the** **Gwich'in** **rural** **communities** **in** **the** **Yukon** **and** **Northwest** **Territories**, **the** **Tlicho** **reserves** **in** **the** **NWT**, **and** **the** **Déné** **Sulpiqué** **reserves** **in** **the** **Northwest** **Territories**), **and** **in** **the** **urban** **centers** (**the** **Indigenous** **population** **of** **Whitehorse**, **Yellowknife**, **and** **the** **BC** **interior** **cities**).

### Seams (DCI line 05)
- **With the Plateau peoples (I2):** The **Dene** **peoples** **border** **the** **Plateau** **peoples** **at** **the** **southeastern** **edge** **of** **the** **Dene** **territory**, **and** **they** **share** **a** **dependency** **on** **the** **salmon** **runs** **and** **the** **plateau** **river** **systems**.
- **With the Great Basin edge peoples (I5):** The **Great** **Basin** **peoples** **border** **the** **Dene** **territory** **at** **the** **western** **edge**, **and** **they** **share** **a** **dependency** **on** **the** **root** **crops** **and** **the** **game** **of** **the** **nearby** **plateau** **and** **desert** **fringe**.
- **With the settler units:** The **Dene** **peoples** **are** **overlaid** **by** **the** **settler** **units** **of** **the** **BC** **interior** **and** **El** **Norte** (S4), **but** **the** **seams** **are** **not** **clean** **lines** — **the** **reservations** **are** **co-located** **with** **the** **settler** **towns** (**the** **Dakelh** **reservations** **in** **the** **BC** **interior**, **the** **Tlicho** **reserves** **in** **the** **NWT**), **and** **the** **Indigenous** **population** **is** **present** **in** **the** **urban** **centers** (**the** **Indigenous** **population** **of** **Whitehorse**, **Yellowknife**, **and** **the** **BC** **interior** **cities**).
