## Findings

### Founding Population and When (DCI line 01)
- The **Yuman** peoples' **territories** were **occupied **for** **thousands** **of years** **before** **European** **contact** — the **Yuman** **language** **family** **is** **part** of the **Hokan** **stock**, **with** **the** **Yuman** **speakers** **having** **migrated** **to** **the** **Colorado** **River** **basin** **and** **the** **California** **desert** **fringe** **at** **least** **1,000** **years** **before** **European** **contact**.
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **two** **major** **divisions**: the **river** **Yumans** (the **Mojave**, **Halchidhoma** (Pilaga), **Yuma** (Quechan), **Cocopa**, and the **Maricopa** of the **middle Gila**) and the **upland** **Yumans** (the **Hualapai** (Walapai), **Havasupai**, and **Yavapai**).
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **are** **also** **closely** **related** **to** **the** **Kumeyaay**, **Tipai**, **Ipai**, **Kiliwa**, **Paipai**, and **Cocopa** **of** **the** **California** **desert** **fringe** **and** **northern** **Baja** **California** — **the** **Tipai** **and** **Ipai** **are** **closely** **related** **to** **the** **Kumeyaay** **of** **Southern** **California**.
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **were** **the** **first** **peoples** **to** **encounter** **the** **Spanish** **explorers** **in** **the** **1760s** **and** **1770s** (**the** **Yuma** **and** **the** **Kumeyaay** **of** **the** **Colorado** **River** **delta** **were** **the** **first** **to** **encounter** **the** **Spanish** **explorers** **in** **the** **1760s**).

### The Governing Frame (DCI line 02)
- The **Yuman** **culture** is **defined** **by** **the** **Colorado** **River** **and** **the** **desert** **fringe** **of** **the** **California** **coast** — **the** **river** **Yumans** **were** **primarily** **farmers** **who** **lived** **in** **the** **Colorado** **and** **Gila** **river** **valleys**, **taking** **advantage** **of** **the** **annual** **floods** **that** **deposited** **rich** **silt** **on** **the** **flood** **plain**, **with** **irrigation** **being** **unnecessary** **in** **what** **was** **otherwise** **an** **arid** **environment**.
- The **upland** **Yumans** (**Hualapai**, **Havasupai**, **Yavapai**) **traditionally** **engaged** **in** **some** **farming**, **but** **a** **major** **part** **of** **subsistence** **was** **based** **on** **hunting** **and** **gathering** **wild** **foods**.
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **a** **loose** **form** **of** **politicism** **rather** **than** **a** **centralized** **authority** — **they** **lived** **in** **dispersed** **settlements** **or** **hamlets**, **with** **no** **chiefly** **class** **or** **centralized** **political** **structure**.
- The **Yuman** **religion** **was** **characterized** **by** **belief** **in** **a** **supreme** **creator**, **faith** **in** **dreams**, **and** **use** **of** **song** **narratives** **in** **ritual** **and** **ceremony** — **the** **Bird** **Song** **singers** **and** **dancers** **were** **the** **key** **ceremonial** **group** **of** **the** **lower** **Colorado** **River** **Yumans** (the **Quechan**, **Cocopa**, **Maricopa**, **Halchidhoma**), **with** **the** **Bird** **Song** **being** **a** **lengthy**, **complex** **song** **cycle** **that** **spoke** **of** **origin** **stories** **and** **other** **oral** **traditions**.
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **interaction** **with** **the** **Colorado** **River** **and** **its** **resources** — **their** **culture** **is** **deeply** **rooted** **in** **the** **river** **and** **the** **desert** **fringe**.

### Territory Within the Target (DCI line 03)
- The I4 unit is the **Yuman** **speaking** **peoples'** **territory** — the **Colorado** **River** **basin** **and** **the** **California** **desert** **fringe**, **including** **the** **Colorado** **River** **delta**, **the** **lower** **Colorado** **River** **valley**, **the** **Mojave** **Desert**, **the** **California** **coast**, **and** **the** **northern** **Baja** **California** **peninsula**.
- Bounded by the **California peoples** (I3) **to** **the** **west**, the **Plateau** **peoples** (I2) **to** **the** **northeast**, the **Great Basin edge** **peoples** (I5) **to** **the** **east**, and the **settlements** **of** **El** **Norte** (S4) **and** **Sudcalifornia** (S5) **to** **the** **south**.

### Present-Day Demographics (DCI line 04)
- The **Yuman** **peoples** **today** **are** **politically** **organized** **into** **tribe/band** **governments** (the **Quechan**, **Cocopa**, **Mojave**, **Walapai** (Hualapai), **Havasupai**, **Yavapai**, **Kumeyaay**, **Tipai**, **Ipai**, **Kiliwa**, **Paipai**, **Maricopa**, **Halchidhoma**, **Yavapai**, **Kawaiisu**, **Luiseño**, **Cupeño**, **Cahuilla**), **with** **land** **claims** **and** **self** **government** **agreements** **being** **a** **major** **political** **feature** **of** **the** **region**.
- The **Indigenous** **population** **is** **concentrated** **in** **the** **reservations** **and** **rural** **communities** (the **Fort** **Yuma** **Quechan** **Reservation** **on** **the** **Colorado** **River** **bordering** **California** **and** **Baja** **California**, **Mexico**; **the** **Colorado** **River** **Indian** **Tribes** **land** **on** **both** **sides** **of** **the** **Colorado** **River** **in** **Arizona** **and** **California**; **the** **Cocopa** **Reservation** **on** **the** **lower** **Colorado** **River**; **the** **Kumeyaay** **reservations** **in** **Southern** **California**; **the** **Walapai** **and** **Havasupai** **reservations** **in** **Arizona**).
- The **Yuman** **population** **is** **weathered** — **the** **Spanish** **missions** **and** **the** **later** **American** **settlers** **drastically** **reduced** **the** **population**, **but** **there** **has** **been** **a** **recovery** **in** **recent** **decades**.

### Seams (DCI line 05)
- **With the California peoples (I3):** The **Yuman** **peoples** **border** **the** **California** **peoples** **at** **the** **inland** **desert** **fringe** **of** **Southern** **California** **and** **the** **Colorado** **River** **delta**, **with** **the** **Kumeyaay**, **Tipai**, **Ipai**, **and** **Cocopa** **being** **the** **closest** **to** **the** **California** **peoples** **at** **the** **Colorado** **River** **delta** **and** **the** **desert** **fringe**.
- **With the Great Basin edge peoples (I5):** The **Mojave** **and** **Colorado** **Desert** **Yumans** **border** **the** **Great** **Basin** **Indigenous** **peoples** **at** **the** **southern** **edge** **of** **the** **Great** **Basin**, **and** **they** **share** **a** **dependency** **on** **the** **Colorado** **River** **and** **the** **desert** **resources**.
- **With the settler units (S4, S5):** The **Yuman** **peoples** **are** **overlaid** **by** **the** **settlement** **units** **of** **El** **Norte** (S4) **and** **Sudcalifornia** (S5), **but** **the** **seams** **are** **not** **clean** **lines** — **the** **reservations** **are** **co-located** **with** **the** **settler** **towns** (**the** **Fort** **Yuma** **Quechan** **Reservation** **on** **the** **Colorado** **River** **bordering** **California** **and** **Baja** **California**, **Mexico**), **and** **the** **Indigenous** **population** **is** **present** **in** **the** **urban** **centers** (**the** **Indigenous** **population** **of** **Los** **Angeles**, **San** **Diego**, **Tijuana**, **and** **the** **border** **towns**).
