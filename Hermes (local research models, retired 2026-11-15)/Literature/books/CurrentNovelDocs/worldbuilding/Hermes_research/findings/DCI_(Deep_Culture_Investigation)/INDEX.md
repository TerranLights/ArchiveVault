# Hermes research: index

One row per findings file. Hermes adds the row when it finishes a file; Claude adds the standing (see section 7 of `/home/kuroskalacs/Documents/Doll-Fi/media/HERMES_RULES.md`).

## 1. Structure

```
DCI findings root: /home/kuroskalacs/Documents/Doll-Fi/media/Literature/books/CurrentNovelDocs/worldbuilding/Hermes_research/findings/DCI_(Deep_Culture_Investigation)/
  INDEX.md (this file)
  <Continent>/          (e.g. North_America, Europe, East_Asia — 10 continents)
    <Country>/          (e.g. Cascadia, Japan, Germany — 83 countries)
      <Zodiac>/         (e.g. 01 - Aries, 02 - Taurus — 12 sign folders per country)
        <Concept>.md    (each concept finding file)
```

## 2. Scale

- **83 targets** (countries/cultures/nations) in Tepenia, from `DCI countries _ cultures checklist [Hermes].md` (Part C)
- **12 signs** (Zodiac meta-categories), from `Shared/Avenues/01 - 12 - Pisces.md` and the other 11 sign files
- **~10–12 concepts per sign** (varies by sign; see Section 3)
- **~9,960–10,000+ finding files** expected across all targets
