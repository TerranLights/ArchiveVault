## Findings

### Founding Population and When (DCI line 01)
- The **Great Basin** **peoples'** **territories** were **occupied **for** **thousands** **of years** **before** **European** **contact** — the **Great Basin** **culture** **area** **is** **one** **of** **the** **oldest** **continuously** **occupied** **regions** **in** **North** **America**, **with** **evidence** **of** **human** **presence** **dating** **back** **to** **the** **late** **Pleistocene** **and** **early** **Holocene** (**c.** **10,000**–**12,000** **years** **ago**).
- The **Great Basin** **culture** **area** **is** **a** **high** **basin** **and** **mountain** **region** **between** **the** **Sierra** **Nevada** **and** **the** **Rocky** **Mountains**, **covering** **parts** **of** **Nevada**, **Utah**, **Oregon**, **California**, **and** **Idaho**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **a** **harsh**, **arid** **environment** **with** **low**, **unpredictable** **rainfall**, **extreme** **temperatures** **in** **summer** **and** **winter**, **and** **a** **delicate** **ecosystem** **of** **sagebrush**, **grasses**, **pinyon**, **and** **juniper**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples** **are** **organized** **into** **bands** **of** **hunters** **and** **gatherers** **who** **moved** **with** **the** **seasonal** **rounds**, **following** **the** **Pinyon** **pine** **nuts** **in** **the** **fall**, **the** **deer** **and** **antelope** **in** **the** **winter**, **and** **the** **seeds** **and** **roots** **in** **the** **spring** **and** **summer**.

### The Governing Frame (DCI line 02)
- The **Great Basin** **culture** **is** **defined** **by** **a** **high** **degree** **of** **mobility** **and** **a** **low** **degree** **of** **sedentism** — **no** **fixed** **villages**, **no** **fixed** **territory**, **and** **no** **centralized** **authority** — **the** **bands** **moved** **through** **a** **tribal** **area** **or** **territory** **that** **was** **not** **a** **fixed** **or** **enclosed** **space**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples** **rely** **on** **a** **wide** **variety** **of** **wild** **foods** — **the** **seeds**, **nuts** **(grew** **the** **seeds** **of** **the** **pinon** **pine**), **roots**, **berries**, **the** **seeds** **of** **the** **bulrush**, **the** **seeds** **of** **the** **cattail**, **the** **game** **of** **deer**, **antelope**, **rabbit**, **squirrel**, **birds**, **and** **fish**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples** **are** **known** **for** **their** **basketry** — **the** **pine** **nuts** **were** **not** **only** **a** **food** **source** **but** **a** **trade** **good** **that** **was** **exchanged** **for** **other** **goods** **from** **farther** **away**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples'** **religion** **was** **characterized** **by** **belief** **in** **spirits** **and** **supernatural** **forces** **that** **inhabited** **the** **natural** **world** — **the** **animals**, **plants**, **rocks**, **and** **water** **sources** **were** **all** **believed** **to** **have** **spirits** **or** **personalities**.
- The **Great Basin** **peoples** **are** **defined** **by** **their** **interaction** **with** **the** **harsh** **environment** **of** **the** **Great** **Basin** **— **their** **culture** **is** **a** **culture** **of** **survival** **and** **adaptation** **to** **a** **difficult** **environment**.

### Territory Within the Target (DCI line 03)
- The I5 unit is the **Great Basin edge** and its **surrounding** **ecosystems** — the **Great Basin** **interior**, **the** **Sierra** **Nevada** **eastern** **foothills**, **the** **Great** **Salt** **Lake** **basin**, **and** **the** **eastern** **Great** **Basin** **margins**, **including** **the** **Nevada** **Great** **Basin** **BLM** **lands**, **the** **Owens** **Valley**, **the** **Mono** **Lake**, **and** **the** **Colorado** **Plateau** **edge**.
- Bounded by the **California peoples** (I3) **to** **the** **west**, the **Yuman** **peoples** (I4) **to** **the** **south**, the **Plateau** **peoples** (I2) **to** **the** **northeast**, and the **settlements** **of** **El** **Norte** (S4) **and** **Sudcalifornia** (S5) **to** **the** **south**.

### Present-Day Demographics (DCI line 04)
- The **Great Basin** **Indigenous** **population** **today** **is** **estimated** **at** **several** **hundred** **thousand** **people** **across** **the** **region**.
- The **peoples** **are** **politically** **organized** **into** **band/tribe** **governments** (the **Western** **Shoshone**, **Northern** **Shoshone**, **Eastern** **Shoshone** (the **Wind** **River** **Reservation** **Shoshone**), **Northern** **Paiute**, **Southern** **Paiute**, **Owens** **Valley** **Paiute**, **Mono** **Paiute**, **Kawaiisu**, **Timbi**-**Shah**, **Gosiute**, **Uinta**, **Düüdüca** (the **White** **Pine** **Shoshone**), **Honni** (the **Panamint** **Shoshone**), **the** **Koso** (the **Monachi** **Shoshone**), **the** **Pacifico** (the **Delamar** **Shoshone**), **and** **the** **Wadadüka** (the **Wadadüka** **Shoshone**)), **with** **land** **claims** **and** **self** **government** **agreements** **being** **a** **major** **political** **feature** **of** **the** **region**.
- The **Indigenous** **population** **is** **concentrated** **in** **the** **reservations** **and** **rural** **communities** (the **Western** **Shoshone** **reservations** **in** **Nevada** **and** **Utah**, the **Northern** **Paiute** **and** **Owens** **Valley** **Paiute** **rural** **communities** **in** **Nevada** **and** **Oregon**, the **Eastern** **Shoshone** **reservation** **in** **Wyoming**), **and** **in** **the** **urban** **centers** (**the** **Indigenous** **population** **of** **Las** **Vegas**, **Reno**, **Springfield** **(Oregon)**, **and** **the** **California** **cities**).

### Seams (DCI line 05)
- **With the California peoples (I3):** The **Great** **Basin** **peoples** **border** **the** **California** **peoples** **at** **the** **western** **edge** **of** **the** **Great** **Basin**, **the** **Owens** **Valley** **Paiute**, **the** **Mono** **Paiute**, **and** **the** **Kawaiisu** **being** **the** **closest** **to** **the** **California** **peoples**.
- **With the Yuman peoples (I4):** The **Mojave** **and** **Colorado** **Desert** **peoples** **border** **the** **Great** **Basin** **Indigenous** **peoples** **at** **the** **southern** **edge** **of** **the** **Great** **Basin**, **and** **they** **share** **a** **dependency** **on** **the** **desert** **resources**.
- **With the Plateau peoples (I2):** The **Great** **Basin** **peoples** **border** **the** **Plateau** **peoples** **at** **the** **northeastern** **edge** **of** **the** **Great** **Basin**, **and** **they** **share** **a** **dependency** **on** **the** **root** **crops** **and** **salmon** **runs** **of** **the** **nearby** **plateau** **and** **river** **systems**.
- **With the settler units (S4, S5):** The **Great** **Basin** **peoples** **are** **overlaid** **by** **the** **settlement** **units** **of** **El** **Norte** (S4) **and** **Sudcalifornia** (S5), **but** **the** **seams** **are** **not** **clean** **lines** — **the** **reservations** **are** **co-located** **with** **the** **settler** **towns** (**the** **Owens** **Valley** **Paiute** **reservations** **in** **California**, **the** **Western** **Shoshone** **reservations** **in** **Nevada**), **and** **the** **Indigenous** **population** **is** **present** **in** **the** **urban** **centers** (**the** **Indigenous** **population** **of** **Las** **Vegas**, **Reno**, **and** **Springfield**).
